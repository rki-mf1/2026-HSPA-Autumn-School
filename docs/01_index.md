# HSPA Autumn School 2026: Mpox ONT Sequencing and Bioinformatics

The **Health Security Partnership in Africa (HSPA)** project is organizing a two-week training workshop on **Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) and bioinformatics** under **Work Area 6**, which focuses on strengthening genomic surveillance capacity.

The training will take place from **5 October to 16 October 2026** at the **Robert Koch Institute in Berlin, Germany**.

The workshop will focus on laboratory sequencing of the **mpox virus** using the **Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT)** platform and the subsequent bioinformatic analysis of the generated sequencing data.

During the first week, participants will be introduced to the laboratory workflow for mpox virus sequencing, complemented by lectures covering the virology, epidemiology, and diagnostic approaches for mpox. The second week will concentrate on the bioinformatic processing, analysis, interpretation, and reporting of sequencing data.

By the end of the workshop, participants will have acquired the practical and analytical skills required to generate and analyze mpox genomic data and to effectively use sequencing results to support public health surveillance and reporting.

## Target group and pre-requirements

This workshop is intended for participants from **HSPA partner countries** who are involved in supporting public health laboratory activities in the areas of **molecular diagnostics and/or genomic sequencing**.

It is designed for professionals seeking to strengthen their capacity in laboratory-based pathogen sequencing and genomic data analysis for public health surveillance.

Participants should ideally possess:

- Practical experience in molecular diagnostics
- A working proficiency in English
- An interest in bioinformatics and genomic data analysis
- Basic familiarity with command-line tools

---

## Workshop content

### 🧪 Week 1 — Wet-lab

During the wet-lab week, participants will gain hands-on experience in the key stages of mpox virus sequencing using the **Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT)** platform.

The programme includes:

- Mpox sequencing workflow and wet-lab setup
- Library preparation
- Sequencing run setup
- Operation of the sequencing platform
- Monitoring sequencing performance and output
- Troubleshooting and initial review of sequencing data

### 💻 Week 2 — Bioinformatics

During the dry-lab week, participants will work with ONT sequencing data using **Linux-based command-line tools** and established analytical workflows.

Practical exercises will cover:

- Data organization and ONT sequencing file formats
- Quality control and preprocessing of ONT sequencing reads
- Mpox genome reconstruction
- Consensus sequence generation
- Genome quality assessment
- Lineage or clade assignment
- Comparative genomics
- Phylogenetic analysis
- Interpretation and reporting of genomic data for public health applications

---

## 🎯 Learning objectives

Upon completion of the workshop, participants will be able to:

- Explain the principles and applications of **Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT) sequencing** for mpox genomic surveillance
- Perform the key steps of the mpox sequencing workflow, including **library preparation, sequencing run setup, and run monitoring**
- Organize and manage sequencing data and associated metadata for downstream analyses
- Apply basic **Linux command-line tools** for bioinformatics workflows
- Conduct quality control and processing of ONT sequencing data
- Generate and evaluate **consensus genome sequences**
- Perform **lineage or clade assignment**
- Conduct **phylogenetic analyses**
- Interpret sequencing results in the context of **public health surveillance and outbreak investigations**
- Apply the acquired laboratory and bioinformatics skills to support **mpox genomic surveillance activities** in their home institutions
